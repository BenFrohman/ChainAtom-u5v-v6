# Steenbrink spectrum of the chain atom

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman  
License: Apache-2.0

\(W=u^5v+v^6\). \(\mu=25\). Spectrum:

$$
-\tfrac23,\;
-\tfrac12^{\times 2},\;
-\tfrac13^{\times 3},\;
-\tfrac16^{\times 4},\;
0^{\times 5},\;
\tfrac16^{\times 4},\;
\tfrac13^{\times 3},\;
\tfrac12^{\times 2},\;
\tfrac23.
$$

$$
\det(tI-h_*)=(t^6-1)^4(t-1).
$$
